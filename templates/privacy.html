Privacy Policy & Data Protection

Last Updated: September 2026 | ISO 27001 & 256-Bit SSL Encrypted

1. Information We Collect

We collect account profile information (name, phone number, email address), service booking location details, payment transaction records, and communication timestamps required to dispatch technicians accurately.

2. How Your Location Data is Used

Service address information is shared only with the assigned service provider after appointment confirmation to enable route navigation and timely arrival. Exact coordinates are never sold or made public.

3. Payment Security & Encryption

We do not store credit/debit card numbers or bank PINs on our servers. All financial transactions are secured through Razorpay's PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant payment gateway with end-to-end 256-bit encryption.

4. Data Retention & Your Rights

You may review, modify, or request deletion of your personal account data at any time by contacting our privacy compliance officer at privacy@skillsetgo.com.
