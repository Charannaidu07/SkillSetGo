Frequently Asked Questions

When you request a service, you can propose your initial expected budget. Verified service providers in your neighborhood review your request and can either accept directly or submit a reasonable counter-offer. You can negotiate in real time, and once both sides agree on the price, the appointment is confirmed!

All online payments are securely processed through Razorpay and held in SkillSetGo escrow. Your money is never released to the service provider until the service is fully completed and you share the secret 6-digit OTP at your doorstep.

The completion OTP is your safety safeguard. Once the technician finishes the job to your complete satisfaction, you provide the 6-digit code displayed on your Appointments page. When the technician enters this OTP in their work queue, the appointment is marked complete.

You can cancel any booking free of charge before the service provider arrives at your location. If you paid online via Razorpay, the full refund is automatically credited back to your original payment method within 3-5 business days.

Every service professional undergoes mandatory government ID verification, in-person trade skill assessment, background security checks, and carries an official SkillSetGo digital badge with a verifiable QR code.
