Terms of Service & Platform Guidelines

Last Updated: September 2026 | Applicable to all Users & Partner Pros

1. Overview & Platform Role

SkillSetGo operates an interactive digital marketplace facilitating connections between autonomous verified service professionals ("Providers") and homeowners/consumers ("Users"). By accessing or utilizing the platform, you agree to these Terms of Service.

2. Dynamic Price Bargaining & Fair Dealings

The SkillSetGo dynamic bargaining feature allows Users and Providers to negotiate fair pricing. Once both parties accept a final price quote through the system, it represents a binding service agreement. Providers agree never to solicit unofficial off-platform cash surcharges beyond the agreed scope.

3. Payments, Razorpay Escrow & Completion OTP

All online transactions are held in escrow via Razorpay until the service is delivered. Escrow funds are released to the service provider exclusively when the customer provides their confidential 6-digit Completion OTP at the doorstep upon satisfactory service inspection.

4. Cancellation & Refund Policy

  • Free Cancellation: Bookings cancelled prior to the service provider reaching the doorstep receive a 100% full refund with zero cancellation fee.
  • Refund Processing: Refunds for online payments are processed instantly to the original payment source within 3-5 business days.
  • Provider No-Show: If a provider fails to arrive within 45 minutes of the appointment window, an alternative pro will be dispatched with a ₹150 apology credit.

5. SkillSetGo Shield Protection Plan & Warranty

All appointments booked through SkillSetGo include 30-day rework protection. In the rare event of verified accidental damage caused during service execution, SkillSetGo covers up to ₹10,000 under the Shield Protection Guarantee subject to claim verification.
